### 👨‍💻 About Me

Software and subsystems developer, building web and mobile applications for telemetry and data management. I work end to end, from Flutter and React Native interfaces to APIs and integrations on the back end with Node.js and TypeScript, on AWS and SQL/NoSQL databases.

Currently at **Stara S/A** (agricultural machinery and implements), where I build the apps, APIs and telemetry integrations behind the company's machines, including work with international partners such as FieldView / Bayer. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Universidade de Passo Fundo (UPF), and I speak Portuguese (native) and English (fluent).

My undergraduate thesis, LeafScope, is a cross-platform app for offline leaf damage measurement using classical computer vision techniques (HSV segmentation, Otsu thresholding, morphological operations, and Convex Hull). It was tested and validated by professionals in the field of Agricultural Sciences.

I'm interested in algorithms and optimization, and I enjoy exploring automation and simulation strategies, such as grid trading bots and DCA in the crypto space.
